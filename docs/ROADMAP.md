# Roadmap

The goal is the whole game, all 27 levels, with the look of the modern Ratchet & Clank games:
everything remade. We start with two levels, Aranos (tutorial, level 0) and Oozla (level 1), on the
PAL disc, and finish each step on both before starting the next. The rules, texture generators and
tools made for those two are then reused for the other 25 (M8). Each step ends with a test on real
hardware ([TESTA.md](TESTA.md)).

| | Step | What you see | Needs |
|---|---|---|---|
| **M0** | **Foundation** (0.1.0): repository, content rules, CI, the mod installable from a link | the mod in the launcher, a line in the game's log | nothing |
| M1 | **Baseline and inventory:** reference screenshots with OpenRAC's Enhanced graphics off and on; every surface of the two levels catalogued (names only, nothing from the disc) | a list of what each level is made of, and the order to remake it in | the tester's export of levels 0 and 1, shared privately |
| M2 | **Materials and light, Aranos:** metal, glass, emissive panels and foliage assigned and tuned; authored lights; recommended Enhanced settings | Aranos lit and shaded like a modern game, on its original models | M1 |
| M3 | **Materials and light, Oozla:** the same for the swamp, its water and the Megacorp store | the same for Oozla | M1 |
| M4 | **New textures:** our own high-resolution textures for both levels' surfaces (hand-made or generated by scripts in this repository, never upscaled game pixels) | sharp, detailed surfaces | M2, M3 |
| M5 | **New geometry:** terrain and scenery rebuilt denser, with bevels and new props, on the original collision | modern shapes, no PS2 edges | M4 |
| M6 | **Engine features in OpenRAC:** normal, roughness, metallic and occlusion maps for replacement models and terrain, then whatever M2 to M5 found missing. Sent to OpenRAC as pull requests | per-pixel surface detail | an OpenRAC fork under `dakajod-lab` |
| M7 | **Characters:** Ratchet, Clank and the two levels' enemies and NPCs, remodelled on the game's skeletons. Ratchet and Clank are in every level, so they are made once | modern characters | 3D art (below) |
| M8 | **The rest of the game:** scripts apply what M2 to M5 built (material rules, texture generators, lights, geometry) to the other 25 levels. Then each planet is tuned by hand and tested, in the game's order | the whole game remastered | each level's export, M2 to M5 |

## Art

Materials, lighting, generated textures and geometry processing can be made by scripts in this
repository, and they are reviewed like code. Hand-sculpted characters at a modern level need a
3D artist, or a lot of time. Before M7 the pipeline will be ready for one: skeleton templates
from the export, a checker for weights and UVs, and packing into the mod.
